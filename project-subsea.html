Subsea Gripper Mechanism

MATE ROV Competition: Mechanical linkage and seal engineering.

Overview & Engineering Scope

This project focused on the design and manufacture of an underwater end-effector tailored for competition tasks involving precise payload manipulation at depth.

The assembly uses a parallel 4-bar linkage geometry actuated by a high-torque waterproof servo. Critical design phases included calculating seal tolerance margins, iterating 3D-printed ABS housings, and conducting hydrostatic pressure tests.
